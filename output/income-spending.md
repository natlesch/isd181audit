# Brainerd Public Schools, ISD 181

Income and spending from public reports. Fiscal years end June 30. Independent School District No. 181, Brainerd, Minnesota. District number 0181-01.

Governmental funds took in $121,622,792 and spent $122,515,320 in FY2025. Fund balances fell by $464,821, to $32,634,973.

The general fund took in $95,288,614 and spent $95,885,602. Other financing sources of $427,707 are not revenue. The general fund balance fell by $169,281.

## These totals are different questions

Full-accrual government-wide revenue was $123,211,606 and expenses were $119,395,514. The statutory form's all-funds inflows were $123,774,152 and outflows were $125,329,705. The FY2027 adopted budget shows revenue of $124,059,624 and expenditures of $132,895,117. They are not added to the headline.

Adding these pictures double-counts activity and mixes the fund basis, the full-accrual basis, and the statutory gross basis.
